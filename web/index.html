Queens

A partnership card game for four. Two teams, two decks, first to 2000.

How to play
  • Partners sit across from each other (North/South vs East/West). Each player is dealt 13 cards; one card is turned up to start the discard pile.
  • A meld is 3 or more cards of one rank, or 2 naturals plus a wild card. Only 2s are wild; jokers are not. Wild cards must be outnumbered by naturals.
  • Each turn: draw a card (or take the pile), meld or add to your team's melds, then discard. The first player of a hand must draw.
  • To take the pile your team must already be down, and you need two naturals of the top card's rank (or one natural and a wild). A wild on top, or a rank your team has already melded, blocks it.
  • Empty your hand to win the hand. Cards: 3–9 = 5, 10/J/Q/K/A = 10, 2 = 25, Joker = 50, Q♠ = 100. The winners add their melds; the others subtract the cards left in hand and add their melds. The team that did not deal counts first.
